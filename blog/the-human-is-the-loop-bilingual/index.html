【中英对照】The Human is the Loop：人是循环本身

#AI#Productivity#Mental Health#Reflection#Bilingual

中英对照阅读

I recently stepped away from AI for a few weeks. Coming back, I’m more aware of the unhealthy habits I’d formed using it.

我最近离开 AI 几周。回来后，我更清楚地意识到自己使用它时养成的那些不健康的习惯。

假期归来

It was an end-of-summer vacation, and I was lucky to be swimming in cold rivers, eating one too many s’mores, napping after lunch, etc. There was a lot of indulgence and a break from work and routines. For much of the time it was a break from my laptop and phone altogether, which meant a few weeks without AI.

I didn’t miss it. What I’ve realized these past few days back is how much of my AI use was unnecessary.

这是今年夏末的假期，我在冰冷的河水里游泳，吃了太多棉花糖，午饭后睡午觉。很多时候，完全脱离了笔记本电脑和手机——这意味着好几周没有用 AI。

我没有想念它。回来后这几天，我意识到自己过去对 AI 的使用有多大程度上是不必要的。

打开电脑的那一刻

When I eventually opened my laptop a few days ago, I was greeted with eleven cmux tabs, each with multiple agents paused midway through various efforts. Some professional, some personal, and a lot in between. I also had unread badges on Claude chats across everything from taxes to landscaping ideas to policy doc review.

Is this the 2026 version of having a bunch of tabs open? Maybe, but it feels way worse. Each of these was an initiative I thought I needed help with, or that I wanted to automate or outsource some thinking on. But each is also a little stub of guilt over never finishing things.

前几天终于打开电脑，迎接我的是十一个 Claude 标签页，每个里面都有多个 agent 暂停在各种任务中途。有些是工作相关的，有些是个人项目，还有很多介于两者之间。Claude 聊天上也有未读徽章，涵盖税务、园林设计想法、政策文档审查。

这是 2026 年版的标签页爆炸吗？可能吧，但感觉更糟。每一个都是我当时觉得需要帮助的 initiative，或者是想自动化/外包出去的一些思考。但每一个也是一点点愧疚——永远完不成事情的愧疚。

新的压力出口

Instead of forcing me to triage and focus on what matters most in work and life, it feeds an underexamined belief that I should be able to do more now. And it finds release in yet another agent conversation, another terminal pane, another fork no one asked for, another bullshit markdown output I’ll never read.

AI 没有迫使我 triage（分类取舍），没有让我专注于工作和生活中最重要的事，反而助长了一种未经审视的信念：我现在应该能够做更多的事。然后这一切在又一个 agent 对话、又一个终端面板、又一个没人要求的 fork、又一坨我永远不会读的 markdown 输出中找到了释放出口。

效率的衔尾蛇

I have so many half-baked ideas for tools or services I barely need, sparked by a combo of unaddressed workaholism and techno-optimist wishful thinking. And sure, the little things I’ve made do work. They’re running, doing their little things. But none of it helps anyone, and none of it makes me happier or gives me more free time.

我有很多半成品的工具或服务想法，其实我根本不太需要。激发这些想法的，是未解决的工作狂心态加上技术乐观主义的白日梦。当然，我做的小东西确实能跑。但没有一样在帮助任何人，没有一样让我更快乐或给我更多自由时间。

I also think I have been using agents as a layer between me and the tasks that cause me stress. Instead of just taking on the thing directly, I put an agent in the mix. It’s like a special stuffy or totem that protects me.

我还在用 agent 作为我和让我感到压力任务之间的一层。不用直接承担那件事，而是在中间放一个 agent。就像一个护身符。

Another example: I’d try to set things up to be more automatic, so I could theoretically get work done faster and parallelize a bunch of different tasks at once. But in hindsight, no one was asking for these efficiency gains. Equipped with tools that could theoretically make me a faster, more capable builder, I felt a strong urge to maximize my use of the tools by applying them to the task of… maximizing my use of the tools. It’s reflexive in the worst way, a productivity ouroboros.

另一个例子：我试图把事情设置为更自动化，理论上可以更快完成工作，同时并行处理一堆任务。但回想起来，没有人在要求这些效率提升。有了理论上能让我更快、更能干的工具，我有一种强烈的冲动去最大化利用这些工具——通过用它们来最大化我的工具使用效率。这是反射性的、最糟糕的方式——效率衔尾蛇（productivity ouroboros）。

不是技术本身的问题

I’m not arguing that this technology necessarily has these effects on its users. And I think the format, design, and culture around the tech are a huge factor in the habits I formed.

我不是在说这项技术必然对用户产生这些影响。我认为围绕这项技术的形式、设计和文化是形成我所养成习惯的一个巨大因素。

My hunch is that agent usage is (thankfully) not intrinsically neurochemically addictive in the same way as, say, endless algo feed scrolling. But I do see now that there are some very real dependency and habituation effects.

我的直觉是：agent 的使用（谢天谢地）不像无止境的算法 feed 滚动那样本质上神经化学成瘾。但我确实看到了一些非常真实的依赖和习惯化效应。

谨慎地继续使用

Before switching to writing this post, I did enter a prompt into pi for a work project I’m starting. I gave it a bunch of requirements and suggestions, and told it to look at codebases, wikis, schemas, conversations. Setting it up forced me to catch up and think through the current situation.

在开始写这篇文章之前，我确实向 pi 输入了一个工作项目 prompt。设置它的过程迫使我回顾并思考当前的情况。

The AI isn’t going to hand me the perfect solution. The result is not going to 10x me. But it’s pattern matching and searching across a mess of systems and SaaS products, which I’m not great at and don’t enjoy. It might reveal a few gaps in my understanding of the situation and improve the context I bring into the project.

AI 不会给我完美的解决方案。结果也不会让我效率翻十倍。但它在做模式匹配和搜索，跨着一堆系统和 SaaS 产品——这些我不擅长也不喜欢的事情。它可能会揭示我对情况理解的一些差距。

More important: it’s freeing me up to do human stuff like write and reflect right now.

更重要的是：它让我腾出手来做人该做的事——比如现在正在写的这篇文章和思考。

写在最后

I’m still cautiously optimistic about this tech possibly letting people lead richer, more thoughtful lives. But that’s only possible if we use it on our terms.

我对这项技术仍然谨慎乐观——它可能让人们过上更丰富、更深思熟虑的生活。但只有在我们按照自己的方式使用它的前提下才可能。

We don’t want the human trapped in the agent loop. The human is the loop, and we tag the agent in occasionally, thoughtfully.

我们不希望人被锁在 agent 的循环里。人是循环本身，agent 只是偶尔被叫进来，要带着思考。
